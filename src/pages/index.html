What to expect

The same rules in every pack

01

Attach and play

Pivots sit where a hand or a socket goes. Axes are shared across the whole pack and one unit is always one meter.

02

Light geometry

Hundreds of triangles per asset, not thousands. The faceted look comes from the mesh, so there are no normal maps to pay for.

03

Specs you can check

Triangle count and length are printed next to every asset on the preview sheets, and the store page lists texture sizes and totals.

04

URP first

Built and checked in Unity 6 with URP. Masks are channel-packed in the layout that Built-in and HDRP also read.

About

Small publisher, strict checklist

{{publisher}} is an independent publisher of low-poly game assets for Unity. We make the kind of packs we want to find when prototyping: consistent, light, and honest about what is inside.

How the packs are made. Our production pipeline is AI-assisted. Models are built through scripted Blender workflows driven by AI agents, texture sources are AI-generated and then baked and packed for Unity, and the prefabs are assembled by an import script. Before release, every asset is checked in the engine against fixed limits for triangle count, scale, orientation and texture size. Each store listing says this plainly as well.
