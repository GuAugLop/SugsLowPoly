Privacy

The short version: we count visits anonymously, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you.

What this site collects

This is a static website. It sets no cookies, runs no advertising scripts, embeds no third-party content and has no forms or accounts. Fonts and images are served from the site itself.

Visit statistics

To know which pages people read, the site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visit counter. When a page opens, a small script sends GoatCounter the page address, the page that linked to it, the browser and operating system, the screen size and the country or region worked out from the IP address. GoatCounter does not store the IP address, sets no cookies and does not follow you across sites; to tell a new visit from a page reload it keeps a short-lived hash that it discards after a few hours. We only see totals, such as how many visits a page had, and cannot identify any visitor. Blocking the script leaves the site working exactly the same.

Like any website, it is delivered by a hosting provider, and that provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and time of request, for security and operations. {{publisher}} does not receive or use those logs to identify visitors.

When you write to us

If you send an email, we receive your address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to answer you and to fix the problem you reported, we do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not share it with anyone. Ask at any time and we will delete the conversation.

Purchases

Packs are sold through the Unity Asset Store. Payments, invoices and account data are handled by Unity Technologies under their own privacy policy. {{publisher}} never sees your payment details.
