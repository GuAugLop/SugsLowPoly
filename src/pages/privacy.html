Privacy

The short version: this site does not track you.

What this site collects

This is a static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, embeds no third-party content and has no forms or accounts. Fonts and images are served from the site itself, so opening a page does not contact any other service.

Like any website, it is delivered by a hosting provider, and that provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and time of request, for security and operations. {{publisher}} does not receive or use those logs to identify visitors.

When you write to us

If you send an email, we receive your address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to answer you and to fix the problem you reported, we do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not share it with anyone. Ask at any time and we will delete the conversation.

Purchases

Packs are sold through the Unity Asset Store. Payments, invoices and account data are handled by Unity Technologies under their own privacy policy. {{publisher}} never sees your payment details.
