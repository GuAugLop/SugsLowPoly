Voxel City: Animals

Nine voxel farm animals and pets, rigged and animated, ready to drop in a scene: chicken, duck, rabbit, cow, goat, sheep, pig, cat and dog. Every species comes as an adult and as a baby, and every model has five textures.

A standalone pack: it needs only URP, not Voxel City. The town, the terrain and the people shown with the animals are other Voxel City packs.

Voxel City: Animals is a set of 18 rigged voxel animals: Chicken, Duck, Rabbit, Cow, Goat, Sheep, Pig, Cat and Dog, each as an adult and as a baby (chick, duckling, bunny, calf, kid, lamb, piglet, kitten and puppy). Every model has five textures, with one prefab for each of the 90, and a LOD Group.

The adults have 140 animations and the babies have their own version of each, 280 in all. Every species has its own set (the hen pecks, flaps and lays an egg, the dog sits, barks and fetches, the pig rolls in the mud and finds a truffle) and every animal also turns on the spot, eats, drinks, sleeps, looks around and reacts to a pat, under the same names, so your code can treat them alike. Walking and other travelling clips stay in place by default; 46 of them also come as a copy with root motion.

Drag a prefab into the scene and it stands, breathes and blinks. The VoxelAnimal component plays an action by name, changes the texture and tells you the marked moments of the clips (the egg laid, the ball picked up). Voxel Animal Wander makes an animal live on its own and a baby follow its mother. The skeletons have named bones, one per body part, and sockets for the mouth and the egg, so you can make your own animations in Unity or in Blender with the FBX files.

  • 9 species, adult and baby of each: 18 models with their own skeletons and LOD Groups
  • 90 textures: five per model, each with its prefab, such as Holstein, Hereford, Jersey, Angus and Brown Swiss for the cow
  • 280 animations: 140 for the adults and the babies' own version of each
  • The same names on every animal: turn, eat, drink, sleep, look around and react to a pat
  • In place by default: your code moves the object, and 46 clips also come with root motion
  • One component: Play, StopAction, SetVariant, Speed and the events of the clips
  • Wander and follow: animals that live on their own and babies that follow their mother
  • 8 props for the animations: egg, ball, stick, yarn, truffle, pail, perch and mud
  • Standalone: it needs only URP, not Voxel City

Technical details

Specs

Models18 rigged animals (9 species, adult and baby), from the 14 cm chick to the 1.9 m cow, and 8 props
Prefabs116: one for each of the 90 textures, 18 base prefabs and 8 props, every animal with a LOD Group
Meshes26 FBX, 1 unit = 1 m, Y up. LOD0 from 859 to 7,959 quads, LOD1 from 377 to 2,825
RigsGeneric, one skeleton per model with 28 to 40 named bones, rigid skinning (one bone per vertex), sockets for the mouth and the egg
Animations140 for the adults and 140 for the babies (564 clips in the FBX files, because a pose is split into start, loop and end), in place by default, 46 also with root motion
Materials and texturesOne material per texture on the pack's own URP shader; 90 animal textures up to 1024 × 512 and 8 prop textures
Scripts7 C# files in their own assemblies, in the VoxelAnimalsKit namespace: VoxelAnimal, VoxelAnimalWander, VoxelAnimalProps
DemoOne scene, Demo_Viewer, to try every animal, texture and action with buttons
AudioNone
Made withUnity 6000.4.5f1 and URP 17.4

Compatibility

What you need

Universal Render Pipeline: required. Made and tested with Unity 6000.4 and URP 17.4.

Built-in and HDRP: not supported, because the materials use a URP shader.

No other package: the pack stands alone. The demo scene works with the old or the new Input System.

FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need Voxel City: Animated Town?

No. The pack stands alone and needs only URP. The town, the terrain and the people in the trailer and in some gallery images are other Voxel City packs.

Does every animal come as a baby too?

Yes. Each of the 9 species is an adult model and a baby model with its own skeleton and its own version of every animation, and both have the same five textures.

How do I play an animation?

Call animal.Play("Moo") on the VoxelAnimal component, or press the button of the action in its inspector in Play mode. A pose such as lying down stays until StopAction(). Speed blends idle, walk and run.

Do the walking clips move the animal?

Not by default: they stay in place and your code moves the object. 46 travelling clips also come as an _RM copy with root motion.

Can I retarget the animations or use Humanoid?

No. The rigs are Generic and each clip is made for its model. You can make your own clips in Unity or in Blender with the FBX files: the documentation walks through both.

Does it have sounds?

No. The pack has no audio; the music in the trailer was made for the video only.

Can I use the pack in a commercial game?

Yes. The pack is sold under the standard Unity Asset Store EULA, which allows use in commercial games. It does not allow reselling or redistributing the assets themselves.

Was AI used to make this pack?

Yes, and the store listing will say so too. The voxel models, the skeletons, the animations and the textures were built by an AI model (Anthropic Claude) that wrote the modelling and animation scripts; no text-to-3D or image-to-3D generator was used. The shader, the C# components and the documentation were made by the same process. The publisher defined the species and the list of animations, reviewed them, made the production decisions and decides what is published. The images and video on this page are renders of the pack in Unity; none was made with an AI image or video generator.
