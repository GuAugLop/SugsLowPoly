Voxel City: Seasons

Spring, summer, autumn and winter for your Voxel City town, with one component and one click. Seasons change at once or blend over seconds: snow piles up on the roofs, leaves turn tree by tree and fall, ponds freeze, and each season brings its own weather and props.

Requires Voxel City: Animated Town 1.1 or newer, sold separately. Get it before buying this add-on: Seasons contains no Voxel City files.

Voxel City: Seasons is an add-on for Voxel City: Animated Town and needs it in the project. Add the VoxelSeasons component and the whole town follows the season: leaf and grass colors, blossom in spring, dry patches in summer, orange, red and yellow trees in autumn, and in winter snow on every surface open to the sky (and none under roofs, porches and canopies), bare trees and frozen lakes, rivers and fountains.

A season changes at once or blends over the seconds you give it, from a script (VoxelSeasons.Set(Season.Winter, 5f)), from the Editor window (Tools > VoxelCity > Seasons), which shows it in the Scene view without entering Play mode, or on a year cycle while the game runs. Hold the town part of the way between two seasons for a calendar, a cutscene or a timeline. Light, sky and fog follow the season with Voxel City's day and night.

92 seasonal props (spring 23, summer 20, autumn 24, winter 25) are placed in one click on the buildings' free attach points and on free ground near what fits them, and each one shows only in its seasons. 18 bare trees with LOD Groups stand in for the deciduous trees while their leaves are down. The weather is drawn on the GPU, with no particle systems: showers with wet streets and puddles, a rainbow after the rain, petals, butterflies, fireflies, falling leaves and mist, and falling snow. Nothing in your scenes, prefabs or materials is changed: turn the component off and the town is as it was.

  • One component, one click: the whole town turns with the season, and nothing in your scenes is changed

  • Weather on the GPU: rain, rainbow, petals, butterflies, fireflies, falling leaves, mist and snow, in three quality levels

FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need Voxel City: Animated Town?

Yes. Seasons is an add-on: it turns the Voxel City town through the year and contains no Voxel City files. Get Voxel City: Animated Town 1.1 or newer before buying this pack.

How do I change the season?

Add the VoxelSeasons component, or open Tools > VoxelCity > Seasons and pick a season. From a script, VoxelSeasons.Set(Season.Winter, 5f) blends to winter over five seconds, and VoxelSeasons.SeasonChanged tells you when it changes.

Does it change my scenes or prefabs?

No. Turn the component off and the town is exactly as it was.

How are the seasonal props placed?

One click places them on the buildings' free attach points and on free ground near what fits them. The same town gives the same layout every time, and each prop shows only in its seasons. You can make your own objects seasonal with one component.

Does it have sounds?

No. The pack has no audio; the music in the trailer was made for the video only.

Can I use the pack in a commercial game?

Yes. The pack is sold under the standard Unity Asset Store EULA, which allows use in commercial games. It does not allow reselling or redistributing the assets themselves.

Was AI used to make this pack?

Yes, and the store listing will say so too. The voxel props and bare trees were built by an AI model (Anthropic Claude) that wrote the modelling scripts; no text-to-3D or image-to-3D generator was used. The season profiles, the shaders, the C# components and the documentation were made by the same process. The publisher defined the seasons and the catalog, made the production decisions and decides what is published. The images and video on this page are renders of the pack in Unity; none was made with an AI image or video generator.
